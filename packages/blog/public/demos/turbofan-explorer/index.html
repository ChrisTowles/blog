A personal side project, built from public data

Turbofan Explorer is a personal side project, started on a mobile phone and built primarily from one with Claude Code. It uses only publicly available information: Wikipedia, manufacturer press pages, trade press and NASA educational material, each linked where it is used.

  • Not official. It is not a product of, affiliated with, or endorsed by GE Aerospace, CFM International, Safran, Rolls-Royce, Boeing, Airbus or any other company named here.
  • No proprietary or internal information was used.
  • Not engineering, maintenance or operational data. Geometry, blade counts, cycle temperatures and pressures, sound, events and trend data are approximate, illustrative or synthetic. Do not use them for any real-world decision.
